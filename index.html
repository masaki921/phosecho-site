Quality

Store pages are not translated word for word. We restructure sentences so the Japanese reads naturally, keep your game’s voice, and follow the conventions of well-localized Japanese Steam pages (register, bullet style, how features are phrased). Game titles and your terminology stay consistent across the page. We use AI tools in the drafting process; every page is read and edited by a native Japanese speaker before it reaches you.
